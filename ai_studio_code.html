pH

Ocean Acidification (pH 8.1 → 7.8)

Excess carbon dioxide reacts with seawater to form carbonic acid, stripping ocean waters of carbonate ions required by pteropods, mollusks, and stony corals to build protective skeletons.

Direct Citizen Action

Support local renewable transition initiatives and consume lower on the aquatic trophic chain.

♳

Microplastics in Pelagic Gyres

Over 170 trillion plastic particles circulate in pelagic convergence zones. Filter-feeding cetaceans and planktonic larvae ingest persistent organic pollutants, bioaccumulating toxins across the marine pyramid.

Direct Citizen Action

Install micro-fiber washing machine filters to arrest clothing runoff and eliminate single-use consumer packaging.
