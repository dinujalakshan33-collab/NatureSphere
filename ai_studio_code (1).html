About NatureSphere

NatureSphere is a non-profit biodiversity and sustainable nature exploration platform. Our mission is encapsulated in our motto: Discover Nature. Explore Our World.

We bridge the gap between scientific field biology (IUCN Red List taxonomy, telemetry arrays) and ethical nature travel (national parks, certified eco-lodges, responsible wildlife observation).

Privacy & Scientific Attribution

1. Wildlife Protection Privacy: Sensitive GPS coordinates for endangered taxa are obfuscated by a 25km buffer to prevent illegal poaching.

2. Eco-Lodge Verification: Featured hotels and eco-stays are evaluated for renewable energy, zero single-use plastics, and local community benefits.
